Questline Starter Bonus test

Live experiment After the experiment

Questline is a fictional app where players earn coins for reaching milestones in mobile games. Many new players never finish a first game. Half of new sign-ups now get a Starter Bonus: the first milestone pays out right away. Does it get more players to finish their first milestone?

The only difference: with the Starter Bonus, the 40 coins for level 5 are paid right away instead of being held until level 10.
